Our Dublin Base

The Weekend Blueprint

Historic Promenade

Explore O'Connell Street and Grafton Street historic quarters. Stroll across elegant historic architectural boulevards, boutique shops, and prominent landmarks highlighting central Dublin's timeless character.

Traditional Pub Social

Immerse the group in classic local establishments, legendary live acoustic folk music sessions, and the lively authentic night social rhythm native to Dublin's cultural heart.

The Guinness Storehouse Experience

Tour the world-renowned fermentation hub at St. James's Gate Brewery. Discover the structural glass atrium modeling massive dimensions, explore heritage advertisement art, and raise a toast to Brendi from the Gravity Bar overlooking panoramic city skylines.

Irish Whiskey Tasting

A refined sensory journey showcasing legendary distilling craft inside the historical Jameson Distillery Bow St. complex or the curated exhibitions at the Irish Whiskey Museum.

The Irish Museum of Modern Art

Wander through elite contemporary collections situated beautifully within historical Kilmainham. Uncover modern installations inside architectural monuments rich with heritage.

Dublin Castle Exploration

Inspect the monumental historic heart of old Dublin. Admire sweeping views encompassing majestic state parameters, ancient structural towers, and lush signature landscaping layouts.

Dublin Bay Coastal Escape

Breathe in fresh coastal air along sweeping waterfront scenes. Relax and unwind away from urban tracks via coastal overlooks, beautiful harbor horizons, and fresh maritime elements.
